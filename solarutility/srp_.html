SRP Solar Export Rate Overview

Salt River Project (SRP) currently offers a solar export rate of $0.02810/kWh under its Customer Generation Price Plan. This rate is significantly lower than those offered by APS and TEP, making it one of the least favorable for solar customers in Arizona.

Key Considerations

  • No Net Metering: SRP unilaterally ended net metering in 2015, replacing it with a fixed-rate export model.
  • Demand Charges: Residential customers face commercial-style demand charges that can add over $250/month to bills if not carefully managed.
  • Board-Controlled Rates: Export rates are set annually by SRP's internal boards, many of whom are elected by large landowners with historic agricultural interests.
  • Limited ROI: The low buyback rate and high fixed charges make it difficult for solar customers to achieve meaningful savings without fulltime energy focus.

Understanding SRP's Governance Model

SRP's unique structure dates back to the early 1900s, when large landowners pledged property to secure federal water projects. Today, that feudal caste system legacy continues through a dual-board system where voting power influence is delegated out according to land ownership. This has led to rate plans and pricing policies that favor large agricultural land-owners and some legacy commercial users over residential solar-serf members

What This Means for SRP Solar Members

Installing solar in SRP territory requires a strategic approach. Because of the low export rate and high demand charges, maximizing self-consumption is critical. This usualy means:

  • Investing in energy storage systems, in order to shift solar energy access into optimal (5:PM for dinner) time-of-use demand windows
  • Using smart home technology and control products that manage peak consumption spikes and avoid the associated demand charges
  • Adjusting lifestyle habits and activities to strategically align with off-peak consumption and solar power production

While these strategies can help, they also require some heafty upfront capital and energy management focus if you are going-solar. For most SRP members, their financial impact and return will take significantly longer to realize than an APS or TEP customer with similar energy requirements.
