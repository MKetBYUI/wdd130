A Deeper Dive into Arizona Utility History and Solar Politics

"We didn't start the fire, it was always burning since solar's been churning..."

AZ Solar History Overview

Arizona's Energy Landscape is influenced by a revenue-profitability-driven model that is dominated by three major players: Pinnacle West (APS), UniSource (TEP), and of-course Salt River Project (NFP). These aren't just power producers and distributors, they are energy policy architects that keep close tabs on what the other non-competitive providers are getting away with. Most of the non-competitive energy producers and resellers in Arizona are nominally monitored and regulated by the AZCC, but SRP along with other not-for-profit NFP's operate from behind antiquated governance models, entirely outside of AZCC visibility (reporting) or jurisdiction (enforcement).

Before You Invest in an alternative energy concept, here are a three key points to keep at the front of your mind and close to your checkbook:
1. The vast majority of solar adopters are disappointed, disillusioned or completely frustrated with the financial aspect of their decision. 2. Knowledge is only potential power. It becomes kinetic energy that performs real work when applied with intelligence. That application of knowledge toward making good decisions starts with good information. Having "solar things" work or work out for you requires focussed attention and rational processing.
3. While the legal concepts of conspiracy, anti-trust and collusion are inherently subtle, concealed and silent, that silence does not mean that nothing is going on behind your back. Backroom processing is always clandestine, due to the internal self-interest of the net-working group involved.
With that said, You can still empower your roof no matter the single-source jurisdiction that you live in, but first, you should understand the politi-solar nuances in your area of one-choice jurisdiction.
How much solar you're allowed to install, how or if your system is grid-connected and monitored, how many spy-meters are present, and how it needs to perform within ever-evolving rate plans is managed (mandated) by the non-competitive, revenue-driven framework back at Energy-HQ. Various topology-specific policies and price plans can drive your annual savings down to net-zero, or worse. Especially if you arrive at the party in the typical solar-finance vehicle.

This isn't just a theoretical maybe. It's a real, ongoing erosion of value, and diversion of funds. This anti-competitive situation is partially fueled by a regulatory dumpster fire that the Arizona Corporation Commission started, but has not managed to get under control for their citizen voter constituents. Not withstanding the outliers like SRP, your potential for any level of energy independence, or minimized savings, remains largely dependent on your provider's benevolence, their internal policy-politics and the systemic behind-the-meter monitoring which facilitates valuable insights and future overreach.

Solar Was Supposed to Be Simple, Reliable and Economical: Back in 2009 - 2010, the original solar concept was elegantly straightforward. Your system produces a seasonally predictable amount of electricity on your roof. More during the longer days of summer, less in the shorter days of winter. You set your thermostat at a preferred, comfortable temperature for your stay-at-home cats and you left for work. Note: The most-used internal systems in the typical U.S. home "Auto-Cycle". That is to say that they cycle On and Off and On and Off, at semi-predictable intervals. We are talking about things like heat pumps, pool pumps, water heaters, refrigerators and freezers that can cycle On and would get some great offset contribution from solar, avoiding some power purchases from Energy-HQ. Solar-Assist also includes constant daytime support for low-energy "parasite loads" like appliance clocks, network routers, televisions, garage door openers and device chargers that are plugged in and are "always-on", ready to consume any renewable energy they can utilize while the sun is shining. Any incidental excess energy is automatically passed back through a bi-directional billing meter, through utility wiring, and then it passes forward forward through a neighbors billing-meter. Despite all of the previous rhetoric about excess solar energy "wrecking the grid" (in Hawaii), the reality based on physics is that electricity follows the path of lowest impedance, and that "low impedance path" is through the wires that systemically distribute power/energy to the multiple meters around your neighborhood, which incidentally receive power output from a shared utility transformer.
IOW: Your supposedly destructive, excess solar energy, rarely or never, leaves the cul-de-sac.

Maybe your unused energy helps a neighbor heat some water, cook some food, do some laundry, or keeps a stay-at-home parent and the kids comfortable while you are at work. It is a clean, efficient, seamless exchange. Everyone benefits.
The utility company has less demand on their mid-day production resources, as they move (resell) your excess energy across the street. To which they have zero expense or effort in relation to the production, transmission, or any additional expense of transfer. You excess energy travels a very short hop in terms of utility distances, literally at the speed of light, where it is instantly resold by your friendly, neighborhood, single-source utility company. The energy passes right through your bi-directional smart-meter and through someone else's AC unit. No harm, No foul. The serving utility doesn't generate that solar power. They don't move it very far. They just manage it organically, with wires already buried in place, without additional expense or effort. But that transfer is only a wash for them under the Net-Metering concept. (Therein lies a potential "dumpster fire" problem).

Fast forward to 2013-2014. Utilities are in full-reverse, revenue panic mode, due to the accelerating deployment of the decentralizing, private-energy-production model. Behind the scenes and on public platforms they are screaming hysterically about the "Duck Curve! the Duck Curve!", while pointing to the hot-mess that massive solar adoption in Hawaii was causing for their antiquated, unkept and overworked grid structure that still had some WW2 cobwebs hanging off of their under-equipped, low-tech, vintage distribution system. Meanwhile, Arizona utility companies are commiserating, strategizing and developing more rhetoric in their back-offices, while soliciting the AZCC to stop the bleeding and to help recover diverted revenue. The net utility outcry result was Full Ignition of the AZCC Solar Dumpster Fire. Higher "connection fees", more charges, more surcharges and weird "taxes" that no-one outside of Energy HQ understands, system limitations, export limitations, diminishing buyback tariffs, extra meters for intelligence gathering, and more recently there are outright denials of any net-energy compensation with various electric delivery providers. And don't forget, those smart-spy-meter deployments that track energy intake going from YOUR solar, into YOUR privately owned storage container. Which can inherently provide data for full visibility on consumer energy habits. That visibility helps identify your corresponding energy-usage-windows or time-of-use habits, which is essential data that can be used toward developing future rate plan strategies.

Insights into your behavior and their jurisdictional overreach typically involve the application of smart “spy meters”, behind the billing meter, that actually monitor energy production and storage capacity, and can be used to interpret private usage behaviors with unsettling precision. As an example of how the system works against you: Your ability to purchase fuel (stored energy) for your car or truck is now restricted to a single brand of fuel, based on your address, and there is now a remote telemetry device (monitor) installed into your vehicle that keeps tabs on your driving performance 24-7-365. It tracks how far, how fast and how long you drive. Mostly it is set in place to record your acceleration curves and the highest sustained speed each month. That data (your energy behavior data) is regularly and automatically reported back to Energy-HQ regarding how fast you accelerate, how long you sustained your highest speed for, and how quickly your "bad" driving habits are depleting your fuel in your tank. The more fuel you accelerate through for sustained 30 minute periods, the higher the effective price of fuel will be when you go to pay off that single-source credit card each month.
Keep in mind that we are talking about normal acceleration up to a sustained cruising speed. Let's say it is an equivalent of 40 - 50 mph around town on surface streets, and then onto a freeway for some 60 - 70 mph transit time. Our imaginary example comes from an in-house video that SRP produced, posted and hosted on their site for a long time. It vilified you and your absurd driving (energy) habits as a polite, smiling, but out of touch NASCAR driver, with a helmet and a crash cage tearing through town instead of maintaining a reasonable and prudent driving speed. Net Result: Higher sustained RPMs over time and distance for solar customers, means punitive damages that non-solar members are not subject to.
IOW: It is perfectly fine for non-solar SRP customers to "lay some scratch on the pavement" for a cannonball run, and then "slip it onto the runway for a full-stop landing" multiple times per day, for the rest of their non-solar lives, while renewable energy adopters must maintain school-zone speeds on their daily commute via the freeway.
Would you put up with that ridiculous scenario from Chevron, Shell, CircleK or QT in real life? We wouldn't, So why let the monopoly that sets their fuel prices to their shareholder's, or their employee's maximum available benefit drive your home? There are ways to legally, ethically and morally avoid that co-dependent relationship trap.

Utilities can and do apply punitive commercial style charges that track lower level demand fees for even less than 10kW, ("10kW" is about the normal equivalent of your dryer and your AC unit running at the same time), Go figure. That demand data is leveraged to inflict substantial demand charges against residential customers who dare to go solar. The goals are very clear: Discourage access, Encumber and Inhibit all simple-system processing, to be thoughtfully replaced with a hyper-confusing, anti-competitive, Revenue Recovery Rat Maze (RRRM). The concept of decentralized, sustainable, solar energy savings is done. If not permanently "Deep Six'd", it is for sure locked up in a back-room wall-safe.

Please don't misunderstand. Utility companies absolutely understand the value solar energy, but they also Love to Hate solar energy production on YOUR roof. SRP, a pseudo-municipal-NFP "not-for-profit" entity, has deployed solar rate plans so punitive they should deter any intelligent, rational solar prospect from pursuing sustainable energy alternatives. Their internal politics and policy decisions are designed to reflect 100% of your energy expenditure dollars back-around and into their system whether they centrally produced the power, or not. SRP's solar customer billing behaviors and revenue processing “quack like a corporate duck.”. They helped propagate the infamous “duck curve” hysteria based on the technical difficulties that Hawaiian utilities were having with their 1950's grid architecture, in order to justify their obsession with revenue retention, which is NFP code for an accounting principle known as "profitability". Internal memos allegedly boast about how lucrative their favorite demand-based solar rate plan is. Surplus revenue is easily absorbed by SRP for some very nice employee amenity facilities that are completely unrelated to energy production, transmission, maintenance, or delivery. Excess revenues could/should probably be refunded annually to so-called members, if transparent reconciliation spotlighted and then eliminated internal expenditures for non-energy line items.
