APS Solar Export Rate Overview

APS currently offers an export rate of $0.07619/kWh for excess solar energy sent back to the grid. This rate is updated annually and applies to customers on the Resource Comparison Proxy (RCP) plan.

Key Considerations:

  • Maximize self-consumption in order to realize significant power offset.
  • Battery storage is essential to shifting solar cycle toward usage windows.
  • Annual reconciliation occurs in April for excess solar export credits.
