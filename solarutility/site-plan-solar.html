Overview

Purpose

Provide relevant, consumer-facing information about privately owned residential solar systems that interconnect with the major electrical utilities in Arizona. Across the state, three major utility companies serve the majority of residents: Arizona Public Service (APS), Salt River Project (SRP), and Tucson Electric Power (TEP). These providers not only distribute electricity, they also generate power for Arizona’s synchronized grid—an interconnected system under the umbrella of the Western Area Power Administration (WAPA). Empower Your Roof—Understand Arizona Solar Nuances: Thinking of going solar in Arizona? You’re not alone—and you’re no longer navigating it alone. Across the state, more homeowners are considering solar modules to take an edge off of their energy expense. Most folks are legitimately trying to shrink their dirty-consumer-power footprint. But there is a BIG catch: how much solar you can apply and how your system topology connects to "their" grid depends entirely on who your utility provider is. Arizona is primarily powered by three major utility companies: Arizona Public Service (APS), Salt River Project (SRP), and Tucson Electric Power (TEP). These energy giants not only deliver power to your home but also own and operate their own electrical generation systems. They are also insiders on the Western Area Power Administration (WAPA) grid. A vast network that coordinates synchronized power stability and transfer across multiple states. And while solar sounds simple—generate power on your roof, use what you need, and feed the rest through the transformer lugs to your neighbors across the cul-de-sac. Well, it’s not always that smooth. Depending on where you live and which utility monopolizes your zip-code, you will face variable connection fees, export limitations, financial penalties and possibly draconian demand charges, all for the permission to reduce your dirty-grid-energy footprint. Some providers are regulated by the Arizona Corporation Commission (ACC), while others, like SRP, operate under their own governance models. So what does this mean for you? Whether you’re just considering solar or trying to re-evaluate your previous investment, understanding your utility’s internal rates, polotics and policis is essential. This site is here to help provide clear, consumer-focused information that helps you make smart, empowered choices. When it comes to managing your grandchildren's energy future, knowledge really is power. Start Here.....
