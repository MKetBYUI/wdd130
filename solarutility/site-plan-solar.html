Overview

Purpose

Provide relevant consumer facing information about privately owned residential solar that interconnects with major electrical producer/provider entities in Arizona. There are 3 major serving utilities in the state, which also have electrical generation capabilities that supply energy to a syncronized grid, which is part of the Western Area Power Authority "WAPA" footprint.
Specifically Arizona Public Service-APS, Salt River Project-SRP and Tucson Electric Power-TEP.
Theses three electic energy providers are variable in their approach to regulating, penalizing and compensating home-owners that want to produce some of their own electricity requirement. The fundamental concept of offsetting utility grid power with native, localized energy from one's own roof or backyard is systemically limited, discouraged and even penalized, especially by electric energy producers that fall outside of the control of the Arizona Corporation Commission Utilities Division. Limitations vary between other small community providers and privately-owned resellers, but this site will provide information only as it pertains to the pact of generation moguls associated with WAPA.

Audience

AZ Electric Utility customers considering the adoption of photovoltaic power that want to understand the application, deployment and tariff challenges associated self-produced solar electric energy
