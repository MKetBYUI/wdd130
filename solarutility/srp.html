SRP Solar Export Rate Overview

SRP offers a solar export rate of $0.02810/kWh under its Customer Generation Price Plan. This rate is significantly lower than the similar APS or TEP areas of jurisdiction, which makes self-consumption strategies absolutely essential.

Key Considerations:

  • SRP boards unilatteraly abolised Net-Metering in 2015, which abruptly instigated the "Customer Generation Price Plan". The plan features commercial style Demand-Rates, that can easiy apply more than $250 per month to your bill, as well as Fixed-Price, price-fixed buyback rates that are determined by 24 internal board members, who set on the Association Board and the District Board. Most are elected (some non-voting members would say appointed), by large parcel "land owners" who currently own larger tracts of land, and enjoy very low kWh rates for their agricultural and other commercial interests.
  • This Feudal-Caste type of "landowner" system dates back to the turn of the century when large-tract farmers and ranchers pledged their lands toward the federal U.S. kingdom, as security for much-needed flood-controls, irrigation improvements, diversionary river management, and storage dams.
  • FFWD to February of 2015 - Your caste is set as a homeowner with commercial style Demand-Charges and steep Time-of-Use rates that effectively preserve excess revenues for SRP, by curtailing reasonable annual savings for solar-serf customers.
  • Hyper-focused, Full-Time Energy-Management and/or expensive Battery-Storage systems with lifecycles that are not quite half of their projected ROI window are required if going solar in an SRP District is to be integrated with reasonable utilization and accesible lifestyle comforts.
