SRP Solar Export Rate Overview

SRP offers a solar export rate of $0.02810/kWh under its Customer Generation Price Plan. This rate is significantly lower than APS or TEP, which makes self-consumption strategies even more important.

Key Considerations:

  • SRP does not use net metering; instead, it uses a buyback rate model.
  • Time-of-use rates and demand charges can affect overall savings.
  • Battery storage can help reduce peak demand and increase solar value.
