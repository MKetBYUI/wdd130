TEP Solar Export Rate Overview

Tucson Electric Power (TEP) currently offers a solar export rate of $0.07000/kWh under its Renewable Energy Standard Tariff (REST) export plan. This rate is reviewed annually and applies to new solar customers who export excess energy back to the grid.

Key Considerations

  • No Net Metering: Like APS and SRP, TEP no longer offers traditional net metering. Exported energy is credited at a fixed rate below retail value.
  • Time-of-Use (TOU) Requirements: Most solar customers are required to enroll in TOU rate plans, which charge more during peak demand hours.
  • Annual Export Rate Review: TEP's export rate is adjusted each year based on avoided cost calculations and regulatory input.
  • System Size Cap: TEP limits the size of residential solar systems eligible for export credits, which may affect larger installations.

TEP's Approach to Solar

TEP has taken a more measured approach to distributed solar compared to SRP or APS, offering a moderately competitive export rate and fewer punitive charges. However, the utility still prioritizes grid stability and cost recovery, which means solar customers must be strategic to maximize savings.

What This Means for TEP Solar Customers

To make the most of your solar investment in TEP territory, consider the following:

  • Design your system to closely match your household's daytime energy usage.
  • Use smart appliances and timers to shift usage into solar production hours.
  • Evaluate whether a battery storage expense is worth reducing some peak-hour grid reliance.

TEP's structure offers way more flexibility than SRP's draconian approach and a slightly higher export rate than APS. It is always cheaper to "reduce" than it is to "produce", so with thoughtful planning, solar TEP customers in can probably achieve a decent return time on their investment, especially when paired with energy-efficient habits and technologies.
