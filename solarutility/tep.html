TEP Solar Export Rate Overview

TEP currently offers an export rate of $0.07800/kWh for excess solar energy. This rate is part of their annual Value of Solar Tariff (VOST) and is reviewed each year.

Key Considerations:

  • TEP’s VOST is slightly higher than APS and significantly higher than SRP.
  • Customers are encouraged to size systems to match usage, not overproduce.
  • Battery storage and smart usage timing can help maximize value.

Use the calculator on the home page to estimate your potential savings under TEP’s export rate.
