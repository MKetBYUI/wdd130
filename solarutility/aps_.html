APS Solar Export Rate Overview

Arizona Public Service (APS) currently offers a solar export rate of $0.07619/kWh under its Resource Comparison Proxy (RCP) plan. This rate is updated annually and applies to customers who send excess solar energy back to the grid.

Key Considerations

  • Annual Rate Adjustment: The export rate is recalculated each year based on market conditions and utility costs.
  • No Net Metering: APS transitioned away from net metering in 2017, replacing it with the RCP model.
  • Time-of-Use (TOU) Plans: Most solar customers are required to enroll in TOU rate plans, which charge more during peak hours and less during off-peak times.
  • Self-Consumption Matters: Maximizing on-site usage of solar energy is key to achieving meaningful savings under APS's current structure.

Understanding the RCP Model

The Resource Comparison Proxy is designed to reflect the value of solar energy through the grid, based on avoided costs such as fuel and infrastructure. While it offers a more favorable export rate than SRP, it still falls short of retail value, making strategic energy use essential for solar customers.

What This Means for APS Solar Customers

To get the most out of your solar investment in APS territory, consider the following strategies:

  • Shift high-energy activities (laundry, dishwashing, EV charging) to off-peak hours when rates are lower.
  • Use programmable thermostats and smart appliances to align usage with solar production.
  • Consider battery storage to reduce reliance on grid power during peak pricing windows.

APS's structure is more solar-friendly than SRP's, but still requires thoughtful planning to optimize return on investment. Customers who actively manage their energy use can still achieve strong long-term savings.
