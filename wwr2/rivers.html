San Juan River

These scenic canyon passages are geared toward a beginning level of experience with gentle flows. The San Juan offers relaxing family-friendly trips with significant native-heritage encounters. The 80+ miles between Bluff and Clay Hills traverses both the Upper and Lower Canyons. The ledges, nooks and overhangs in the canyon are lined with petroglyphs, dwellings, graineries and Moki Steps carved into steep sandstone.

Green River

Cutting through remote wilderness in central Utah, the Green River descends into Desolation and Gray Canyons with steep stone cliffs, sunny vistas, and reminders of ancient human presence. The popular 80+ mile stretch from Sand Wash to Swaseys Rapid offers a mix of tranquil waters and exciting Class II and Class III whitewater, great for both newbies and seasoned enthusiests.

Colorado River

Carving its way through nearly 280 miles of awe-inspiring terrain, the Colorado River cutting through the Grand Canyon offers the most iconic rafting experience on Earth. From the put-in at Lee's Ferry, to the take-out at Diamond Creek - River Mile 226, this journey plunges you through a world of towering cliffs, hidden waterfalls, ancient history and the best-swept rapids in North America.
